Headline number plus caption. The anchor of every dashboard card.

```jsx
<MetricBlock size="hero" align="center" value="1,651,045" caption="Assets processed" delta="+32%" />
```

Sizes: `hero` (88px, one per screen, centred at the top of the view) · `lg` · `md` (card default) · `sm`. Always tabular figures; caption below, muted, 14px.
