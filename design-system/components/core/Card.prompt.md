Container for every panel, metric block and content module.

```jsx
<Card label="Monthly exports" dot actions={<IconButton size="sm" variant="quiet" icon="ph ph-corners-out" label="Expand" />}>
  <MetricBlock value="17,2k" caption="Exports" />
</Card>
```

Tones: `raised` (white, default) · `quiet` (#F7F7F8, for cards sitting on white) · `inset` (#E6E6E8, a recessed frame around a mock or media) · `invert` (black — sparingly, at most one per view).
