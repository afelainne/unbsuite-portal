Glyph-only square button — nav rails, card toolbars, header clusters.

```jsx
<IconButton icon="ph-fill ph-folder" label="Workspaces" size="lg" active />
```

- `active` flips it to the black skin with a white glyph — that is how selection reads everywhere in this system.
- `variant="accent"` is reserved for the AI entry point (at most one per screen).
- `badge` adds the 6px mint notification dot.
